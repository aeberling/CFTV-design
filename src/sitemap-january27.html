Funds & Scholarships (primary page)
Funds & Scholarships Page Content
  • Funds & Focus Areas
  • Make Your Giving Last: Explore Fund Options - Work with the Community Foundation to create a fund that aligns with your passions and supports the Teton Valley community now and into the future.
  • Why create a fund?
    • Tailor your giving to your passions (education, environment, arts, health, etc.)
    • Support nonprofits, scholarships, and community initiatives with professional stewardship
    • Build a lasting legacy that reflects your values
    • Start a Fund (CTA)
Fund Types:
Donor-Advised FundFlexible fund to recommend grants to causes you care about over time.
Scholarship FundHelp students pursue education and career goals with a scholarship in your name.
Memorial / Named FundHonor someone special while supporting meaningful community programs.
Agency FundProvides long-term support for a nonprofit organization.
Designated FundSupports one or more nonprofits or projects you select.
Competitive Grant FundPool donations to support vetted nonprofits addressing community priorities.
Affinity / Collaborative FundPartner with others to pool resources around a shared cause.
