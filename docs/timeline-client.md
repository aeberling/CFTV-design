## TCC Site

**End of March** — The TCC site goes live as a static site. Most of the site will be visible, with the exception of nonprofit pages. Donation buttons will redirect to CFTV/akoyaGO until the app is connected.

**May 1–10** — The full TCC site launches with all pages and content except full nonprofit details. The race registration form will be built and hosted through GoDonate, with a link from the TCC site directing users to the form. Claire will build out the initial form and we can assist if needed. The race form is separate from the cart and will remain on GoDonate. If needed, this can extend to May 14.

**May 14** — The nonprofit intake form goes live so organizations can begin submitting their information and marketing materials ahead of the campaign.

**June 12** — The TCC campaign officially launches. The cart goes live with all participating nonprofits connected and all donation flows ready to go.

---

## CFTV Site

**April 1** — The current CFTV website will be updated with redirects to GoDonate for direct donations and Challenger gifts. Fund holder login will redirect to GOfund.

**April 6** — The new CFTV site launches as a static version. It may be a smaller version initially — not every page will be ready, but the core site will be live. Some features like reservations, contact forms, and newsletter signups will redirect to pages on the current WordPress site.

**Mid-May** — The full static CFTV site will be complete with all pages and content live, including embedded donation forms. The static site will remain live through the TCC campaign.

**Mid-July** — The new WordPress version of the CFTV site launches and replaces the static site.

---

## App

**June 1** — The app will be fully built and tested, including all nonprofit information and donation flows. This gives us a buffer before the June 12 campaign start.

**June 12** — The app goes live alongside the TCC campaign launch with the full cart and all donation flows functional.
